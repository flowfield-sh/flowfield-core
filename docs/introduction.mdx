Flowfield is a workspace for you and your coding agents. Discuss what you want to accomplish with a Coordinator, let Workers carry out tasks, and review their results. The conversation, requirements, decisions and delivered work stay together as the project grows.

It runs on your machine and works with your existing repositories and accounts for Codex, Claude Code and Pi. You can choose a different agent for planning, implementation and review.

![A Coordinator conversation beside a populated Task Board](/images/task-board.png)

## From conversation to completed work

Start with a problem, an idea or a set of requirements. The Coordinator helps explore the code, clarify the scope and turn the plan into tasks with success criteria.

When you're ready, prioritize tasks on the board and run the queue. Workers carry out eligible tasks in separate working copies. You can continue planning while they work; Worker and Reviewer questions that need your judgment arrive in Inbox. Planning questions stay in your Coordinator conversation.

A Reviewer checks each result against its task. You inspect the findings and code changes, request another pass when needed, and approve the result you want delivered to your local branch. Reports and files remain available alongside the work that produced them.

## When Flowfield helps

Flowfield is useful when you want to:

- **Keep several pieces of work moving.** Prioritize independent tasks, connect dependencies, and coordinate related repositories from one conversation.
- **Give agents shared context.** Maintain project goals, conventions and specifications in documents that tasks can use.
- **Review outcomes in context.** Find the requirements, checks, questions and changes behind a result before deciding to deliver it.
- **Return to work later.** Pick up the conversation, inspect a task's history, or find a delivered file without reconstructing earlier agent threads.

A task can be a bug fix, a feature, a documentation change or a research report. Begin with something small enough that you can easily judge the result.

## Find your way around

The **Coordinator** conversation stays beside the work. **Task Board** shows priorities and progress, **Inbox** holds questions and results that need you, and **Documents** holds shared guidance. **Files** collects outputs; **Metrics** shows the effort spent on the work.

You can also work with Flowfield from an agent in your own editor or terminal. Follow [Connect an external agent](/mcp-integration) to use the same tasks and results through MCP.
